# AxleVeto Brand System

## Name

AxleVeto combines the physical vehicle axis with an explicit safety decision. It is intentionally sharper than generic “fleet,” “pulse,” or “OS” naming.

## Voice

Direct, operational, and calm under pressure. Never playful around unsafe vehicles. Avoid AI clichés, inflated claims, and compliance theater.

## Visual language

- Deep workshop green `#14221D`: authority and industrial context.
- Signal orange `#F35B32`: a dispatch-impacting exception, used sparingly.
- Verification lime `#C7F36B`: cleared state and cryptographic proof.
- Warm paper `#F4F2EB`: human, tactile contrast against cold fleet dashboards.
- Manrope display typography with Inter for operational density.

## Mark

The icon is a constrained axle/grid inside a circular vehicle boundary, cut by an orange horizontal veto line. It remains recognizable at favicon scale and does not rely on a generic shield, spark, or gradient blob.
